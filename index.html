What happens at a camp

A calm chair, a short chat with the nurse, and twenty minutes.

Every SP Trust camp is staffed by a qualified medical team. You're screened, seated comfortably, and guided through the donation while a volunteer checks in on you — then you're off to rest with a cup of juice and a certificate.

Why it matters

Blood can't be manufactured. It can only be given.

Every day, accident wards, surgery units, cancer patients, and expecting mothers across Tamil Nadu need blood that only a willing donor can provide.

Every 2 min

Someone needs blood

From road accidents to childbirth complications, the need is constant — and rarely predictable.

21 days

Is the shelf life of platelets

Unlike most medicine, blood can't be stockpiled for long. Blood banks depend on a steady stream of new donors, not a stored surplus.

1 in 3

People will need blood someday

Whether for themselves or a loved one — donating today builds the reserve that's there when it's your turn to need it.

Before you donate

Who can give blood

Most healthy adults qualify. A quick check-up at the camp confirms you're fit to donate on the day.

  • ✓ Aged between 18 and 65 years
  • ✓ Weigh at least 50 kg
  • ✓ Haemoglobin level of 12.5 g/dL or above
  • ✓ In general good health, no fever or infection
  • ✓ Last donation was 90 days ago or earlier
Not sure if you qualify? Come anyway. Our medical team screens every donor on the spot — blood pressure, haemoglobin, and a short health history — before you donate. If today isn't right, they'll tell you when it will be.
